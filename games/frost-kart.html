❄ Frost Kart

Race 5 snowy rivals around 4 frosty tracks. 3 laps each. Finish a track to unlock the next one!

  • Drive: Arrow keys or WASD. On phones, use the buttons on screen.
  • Drift: hold Space or Shift (or DRIFT) while turning. Keep holding until the sparks turn blue, then orange, and let go for a mini-boost!
  • Drive over glowing boost pads for a burst of speed. Snow off the track slows you down.
