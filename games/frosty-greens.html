⛳ Frosty Greens

18 snowy mini golf holes. Can you beat par?

  • Drag back from anywhere and let go to putt, like a slingshot. Drag farther for more power.
  • Ice is slippery, sand is sticky, and arrows show hills that roll the ball.
  • Water costs 1 stroke and puts you back where you were.
  • Watch out for sliding sleds, Flurry Wheels and bouncy bumpers. Purple snow globes teleport your ball!
  • Keyboard: arrows aim and set power, Space putts, P pauses.
