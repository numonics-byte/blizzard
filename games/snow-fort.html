❄ Snow Fort Defense

The snow critters are marching to your fort! Build snowy towers to stop them.

  • Tap a dashed spot, pick a tower, then tap it again to build.
  • Snowball Thrower hits one critter. Ice Shard Spinner hits everyone nearby. Frost Fan slows critters down. Icicle Cannon shoots far but slowly.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade it (3 upgrades) or sell it.
  • Earn coins for every critter. Call the next wave early for a bonus!
  • Every 5th wave a big Grumbleflake stomps in. Don't let critters reach your fort: you have 20 lives.

Keys: 1-4 towers, U upgrade, S sell, Space next wave, P pause, F speed
