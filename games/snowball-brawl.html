❄ Snowball Brawl

You and 5 snowy pals in a free-for-all snowball fight. First to 10 hits wins!

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys. On phones, drag on the left side of the screen.
  • Throw: click to throw where you aim, or press Space to auto-aim at the closest kid. On phones, tap THROW.
  • Snowballs: you carry up to 5. Stand still in a snow pile, or hold E / Shift (SCOOP) to make more.
  • Snow walls block snowballs. They crumble after 3 hits. Hold SCOOP next to a broken wall to rebuild it.
  • Getting hit sends you sliding and seeing stars for a moment.
